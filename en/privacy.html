Privacy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Your farm stays on your device

The game needs no account. Your farm progress is stored in the local storage of your browser or of the Android app, and the save is never uploaded. There is no save sync between the browser and the Android app. Clearing browser data or uninstalling the app may remove your progress.

What we record about visits

This site records page views, referring sources, campaign channels, browser information, IP addresses and the country and city derived from them, how long a page stayed in the foreground, and whether an APK download, a web-version click or a game load happened. A download event does not mean the install succeeded. Server access logs also record the requested address, time, status code and IP address.

Anonymous visitor identifier

We keep a randomly generated anonymous visitor identifier in your browser's localStorage to estimate unique visitors, and channel information for the current visit in sessionStorage. Visit analytics do not use advertising tracking SDKs or collect names, email addresses or game saves. The statistics are only visible to administrators after they sign in.

Support receipts and coin delivery

When you submit a support request, we store your contact details, payment note, receipt image, submission time and review result for signed-in administrators to verify. Do not attach unrelated personal information. A random local farm credential stays on your device; only its hash is stored on the server for the daily submission limit and coin delivery. Your full farm save is not uploaded. Coin collection receipts are stored with your local save.

Managing local data

You can view and clear localStorage and sessionStorage in your browser's site settings. Note that clearing them may also remove game progress stored under the same domain. Disabling JavaScript stops page analytics, but the web version of the game also needs JavaScript to run. The offline Android game does not use the analytics on this site.
