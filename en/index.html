BEFORE YOU START

Things you may
want to know first.

What is the difference between Android and the web version?

The gameplay is the same. Once installed, Android can be played offline and is best in landscape. The web version needs no installation, but the first load requires an internet connection and a browser with WebGL support. Each keeps its own local save, and syncing between them is not supported yet.

Is my progress saved automatically?

Yes. The game saves on important actions and when you leave the page, keeping a main save and a backup. Continue on the same browser and device; clearing browser data or uninstalling the app can remove your save.

How do I install the Android APK?

Download the APK on a phone running Android 7.0 or later, open it, then allow the current browser to install apps when the system asks, and finish the installation. On iPhone and iPad, please use the web version.

What crops, buildings and decorations are in the game?

There are 4 vegetables (carrot, wheat, cabbage, pumpkin), 3 placeable field plots, 13 buildings unlocked by farm level (including the boathouse and fish market, which can only be built at a fishing lake), 8 trees and 7 garden decorations. The cost, unlock condition and effect of every item are listed in the field guide.

How do I work the farm?

Tap fields, buildings and the map edge to assign work. On desktop, drag to rotate, scroll to zoom and right-drag to pan; on a phone, rotate with one finger and pan or zoom with two. The help button inside the game is always available.

Is the game itself available in English?

Yes — the game is fully bilingual alongside the website. It opens in your browser's language, and the globe button at the top right of the game switches between English and Chinese at any time; the choice is remembered on your device. Opening the game from these English pages starts it in English directly, and the field guide lists every item in both languages.
