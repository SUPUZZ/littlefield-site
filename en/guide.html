A few notes on the numbers

  • “Growth time” only counts while the soil is wet. Once the soil dries out the crop stops growing until it rains or you water it again.
  • Watering by hand uses 1 charge. You start with 6 charges and each Well adds 6 more; visit the well in the home farm to refill.
  • Harvested crops go into the basket and are sold at the stall in the home farm. Barns, the logistics centre and the group HQ raise the price.
  • Growth and water use differ by biome — see the biome table under “Trees” below.

Where fields come from

  • The home farm starts with 16 fixed plots: 12 are ready to work (6 already tilled), and the rest open at once for 100 G.
  • Every new region you claim brings 16 more plots — except a fishing lake, which has only water and two shore building sites.
  • Limits per region: at most 16 + farm level × 4 fields, 2 + farm level buildings and 60 other items.
  • Once planning mode is on, the 16 plots of a new region have to be placed on the map by you before they become usable.

Building notes

  • Every new region comes with 2 building sites; at a fishing lake both are on the shore. When they run out, place it in another region.
  • Out in the wild you sometimes find a ruined well, barn or cottage — rebuilding on the old site costs only half price.
  • Beds are 1 at the start plus 2 per cottage and 6 per dormitory, with a maximum of 16 friends on the farm.

A few rules in planning mode

  • Check the terrain first: water cannot be built on, and buildings cannot cover the four fixed pieces of the home farm — the farmhouse, well, produce stall and windmill.
  • Finished facilities can be moved but not removed; harvest a plot before you pack its field away.
  • Removing something returns part of its cost, and natural trees only count for a little firewood — rebuilding over and over always loses money.
